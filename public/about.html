About Queen Elizabeth Tourism Centre

Who We Are

Queen Elizabeth Tourism Centre is a premier tourism company located in Katunguru, at the gateway to Queen Elizabeth National Park, Uganda. We specialize in creating memorable and authentic travel experiences that allow visitors to explore the rich wildlife, breathtaking landscapes, and diverse cultures of western Uganda.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional, sustainable, and personalized tourism services that connect visitors with nature and culture while promoting wildlife conservation and supporting local communities.

Our Vision

To become the leading tourism centre in western Uganda, recognized for excellence, innovation, and responsible tourism practices.

What We Offer

  • Wildlife safari experiences
  • Game drives in Queen Elizabeth National Park
  • Kazinga Channel boat cruises
  • Chimpanzee tracking in Kyambura Gorge
  • Bird watching tours
  • Cultural and community experiences
  • Customized travel packages
  • Accommodation and transport arrangements

Why Choose Us?

Our experienced guides, local knowledge, and commitment to excellent customer service ensure that every visitor enjoys a safe, comfortable, and unforgettable adventure. We are passionate about showcasing the beauty of Queen Elizabeth National Park while promoting sustainable tourism and preserving Uganda's natural heritage for future generations.
