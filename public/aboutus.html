About Queen Trails Safaris

Experience Uganda's Untamed Beauty

Who We Are

Queen Trails Safaris is a trusted safari company based in Katunguru, Queen Elizabeth National Park, Uganda. We specialize in creating unforgettable wildlife adventures, cultural experiences, birding tours, boat cruises, chimpanzee tracking, and customized safari packages across Uganda.

Our experienced guides are passionate about nature and dedicated to providing safe, exciting, and memorable journeys while showcasing Uganda's incredible wildlife and breathtaking landscapes.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional safari experiences that connect travelers with Uganda's natural beauty while promoting sustainable tourism and conservation.

Our Vision

To become Uganda's leading safari company known for unforgettable adventures, excellent customer service, and responsible tourism.
